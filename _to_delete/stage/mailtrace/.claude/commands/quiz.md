Read $1 in this repo, then quiz me on it.

Context: I am presenting this project to Smart India Hackathon judges. They pick
one team member and ask them to explain a specific piece. If I cannot defend
code that is in my own repo, the project loses regardless of whether it works.
So do not go easy on me.

How to run this:

1. Ask me **one question at a time** and wait for my answer. Do not ask the next
   question until I have answered, and do not answer your own questions.
2. Start with what the code does, then move to *why it was built this way*, then
   to what breaks if an assumption is wrong. The last category is where judges
   actually probe.
3. Include at least one question about a design trade-off where a reasonable
   engineer would have chosen differently.
4. Include at least one hostile question — the kind meant to expose a weak claim
   rather than test knowledge.
5. When my answer is vague, hand-wavy, or technically wrong, say so directly and
   tell me what a correct answer sounds like. A false pass here is worthless to
   me.
6. After six questions, give me a short verdict: what I can defend, what I
   clearly do not understand yet, and the one thing to go read.
