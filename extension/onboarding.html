Set up Mailtrace

Two short steps, then it works in the background

Mailtrace checks the mail arriving in your inbox and flags what looks like phishing or impersonation — with the reasons behind every verdict, not just a label. Scores appear next to your conversations in Gmail.

Catches what filters miss

Impersonation and invoice fraud that pass every authentication check.

Tells you why

Every score lists the evidence that produced it.

Read-only

It can never send, delete or change your mail.

Analysis runs on the Mailtrace backend you sign in to — the extension never judges a message itself, it only shows the verdict that backend returns. You can disconnect Gmail at any time from the extension popup, or from your Google account permissions.
